Chapter 1 The letter

It always arrives on an ordinary day.

A lease renewal. A new job contract. A fine with a reference number and no explanation. The words are English, technically, but they were written for someone else.

So you read it three times, search half of it, and still are not sure what you just agreed to.

The law is there to protect you. It shouldn't need a translator.

Clause 14 · sample

  1. Should the Tenant intend to terminate this Agreement upon expiry of the Fixed Term, written notice thereof shall be served upon the Landlord not less than sixty (60) days prior to the said expiry,

    DeadlineMoving out when the lease ends? Tell your landlord in writing at least 60 days before.

  2. failing which the tenancy shall be deemed renewed on a periodic basis upon the terms herein contained, mutatis mutandis;

    You mustIf you don't, the lease rolls on month to month, same rules.

  3. and the Landlord shall be entitled to retain from the Security Deposit such sums as are reasonably required to remedy damage, fair wear and tear excepted,

    MoneyThey can keep part of your deposit only to fix real damage, not normal wear.

  4. provided always that an itemised statement of any such deductions shall be furnished to the Tenant within fourteen (14) days of vacant possession being delivered.

    Your rightAnd they owe you an itemised list of deductions within 14 days of moving out.

In short: give 60 days' notice in writing, and expect your deposit back minus real repairs, with a list. Source: Clause 14, lines 1 to 4
