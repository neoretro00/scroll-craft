# LAW5 landing page: brief

**Self-authored under explicit creative delegation.** The user gave the product,
the purpose, the aesthetic and the two colourways in one request and did not ask
for an interview. Their words are quoted where they exist; everything else is an
authored decision and labelled as one.

> "creating LAW5 platform landing page that contain motion and story telling of
> the law and AI correspond as AI assistant. I want the landing page to be in the
> animation style with editorial aesthetics while also look youthful with some
> color play. I want 2 separated design first is white and purple while other is
> black and purple. Moreover this website main purpose is to be AI assistant but
> currently in beta test right now and would be nice if it can be easily to
> understand function as well with good flow design."

## The eight topics

1. **Vibe.** User: "animation style", "editorial aesthetics", "youthful",
   "color play". Authored reading: a magazine feature about the law that has been
   marked up by a friend with highlighters. References: a well-annotated
   textbook, a zine spread, a highlighter set.
2. **Journey (user's words, expanded).** "Story telling of the law and AI
   correspond as AI assistant", then "easy to understand function", then beta.
   Authored sequence: the confusing letter, LAW5 reading it with you, how it
   works, what it helps with, what it will and will not do, join the beta.
3. **Energy curve (authored).** Bright and light at the title page, a small dip
   at the letter, the loudest motion at the translation, calm and clear through
   the method, playful in the topic index, a hard serious turn for honesty,
   warm and quiet at the sign-up.
4. **Feeling, and the one moment (authored).** See the curve below. The moment:
   watching a dense legal clause get highlighted by meaning and rewritten into
   plain words, line by line, as you scroll.
5. **Something no other site does (authored).** The scroll is the reading
   finger: it moves a reading-buddy down a real contract clause, colour-codes
   each line (rights, deadlines, money, duties) and swaps it for plain words.
6. **Distance from premium-minimal.** User: editorial plus youthful colour play.
   Family: editorial, pushed toward playful.
7. **One world or distinct scenes.** Distinct chapters. Editorial was asked for.
8. **Assets.** None supplied. No photography: the world is genuinely
   illustrated (SVG and typography), which is what "animation style" asks for.
   No generation spend.

## Colour play, with a job

Purple is the brand (user's colour, so the taste-floor "AI purple" default does
not apply; there are still no violet-to-blue gradients and no glow). The play
colours are **highlighter inks**, and every one of them means something:

| Ink | Means |
|---|---|
| Lime | A right you have |
| Coral | A deadline or a risk |
| Sky | Money |
| Butter | Something you must do |

They only appear as highlighter marks, stickers and step dots, never as UI
chrome, so the purple accent stays locked.

## Feeling curve

| Chapter | Feeling | Caused by |
|---|---|---|
| Title | Curious, welcomed | A friendly headline over a marked-up contract and a reading-buddy that looks at you |
| 1 The letter | Recognition, a small knot | A real wall of legalese, set the way it arrives |
| 2 Read it together (peak) | Relief, "oh, that is all it says" | Each line lights up by meaning and rewrites itself in plain words under your scroll |
| 3 How it works | Clarity, competence | Four steps joined by a line that draws as you read |
| 4 What it helps with | Possibility, "it covers my thing too" | An index of everyday legal topics that opens into real questions |
| 5 Straight talk | Trust | A hard cut to an inverted ground and plain statements of the limits |
| Close | Invited | A quiet colophon with one form |

**Peak:** "You scroll and the contract literally translates itself, line by line,
in highlighter." Lives in chapter 2, the largest span on the page (3.6 vh).

**Tell-someone sentence:** "It's the site where a lease clause turns into plain
English while you scroll."

**Authored silence:** none. Every screen has content.

## Facts and claims

- No statistics, prices, dates, jurisdictions, testimonials or launch dates are
  invented. The page says "beta" and says what beta means.
- The sample clause and the sample conversation are written for illustration
  and labelled as samples on the page.
- The sign-up form is not connected to a backend. With no endpoint configured it
  says so honestly and keeps the entry in this browser only. Set
  `data-endpoint` on the form to wire it.
- No privacy or data-handling claims are made; add them only once they are true.
